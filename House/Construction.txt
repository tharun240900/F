
FOOTING TO FLOORING :

1) As per the plan marking are done on plot.
2) Excavation (Foundation Digging) Machines or labor dig pits/trenches for Footings, Septic, tank Sump.
3) PCC (Plain Cement Concrete): A thin concrete layer(PCC) is poured at the bottom of footing pits it prevents steel from touching soil.
4) Footing concrete is poured and curred for a week/10 days.
5) Columns/Pillars are made from the footing and curred as well.Foundation/footing pits are closed after curring.
6) Once Columns reach ground level Plinth Beams are made. This Plinth Beams connects the Columns/Pillars.
7) Soil filling done inside Plinth Beams to form a basement.
8) Once the basement is done, concrete is poured on top of the basement and then the flooring is done.


FLOORING TO BUILDING :

1) Columns/Pillars are made from the basement.
2) Slab is made on top of pillars and walls are made.put or leave space for windows and doors.
3) Incisions are made on walls for electrical pipes.
4) Plumbing is the next work.
5) Ceiling is done.
6) Flooring is done at last by puting some tails
7) Painting
